Our Vision

"Empowering youth of all ages to fulfill their dreams and ambitions through unified efforts that create endless possibilities and opportunities."

Our Mission

"Our mission is to promote charitable events year-round, nurturing community fellowship and growth within the greater Jamestown area."

Through our events, we strive to provide for those less fortunate in our community, fostering a spirit of compassion, unity, and support. Founded by Cecil Guerry, the festival has become a cherished tradition representing local culture.
