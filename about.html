About Hell Hole Inc.

Empowering youth and fostering community fellowship in Jamestown since 1971

Our Vision

"Empowering youth of all ages to fulfill their dreams and ambitions through unified efforts that create endless possibilities and opportunities."

Our Mission

"Our mission is to promote charitable events year-round, nurturing community fellowship and growth within the greater Jamestown area. Through these events, we strive to provide for those less fortunate in our community, fostering a spirit of compassion, unity, and support."

Our Story

The Hell Hole Swamp Festival and Hell Hole, Inc. exemplify the spirit of community, dedication, and generosity. Founded on the vision of fostering togetherness, these events have become cherished traditions in Jamestown, South Carolina.

Cecil Guerry's initial vision has not only endured but has flourished, thanks to the commitment of volunteers and the support of the Town of Jamestown. The festival's unique attractions, such as the beauty pageants, the children's tobacco spitting contest, and the Hell Hole Gator Trot 10K race, have added character to the event, making it a true representation of the local culture.

By creating an inclusive space where people from diverse backgrounds can come together, socialize, and enjoy an array of activities, these events have not only provided entertainment but also strengthened the bonds of community.

Hell Hole, Inc.'s expansion of community involvement is commendable. By organizing events like the Oyster Roast and supporting initiatives like the Back to School Bash and Halloween Munster Mash, they have gone above and beyond in their efforts to give back to the community. Offering these events free of charge demonstrates a commitment to inclusivity, ensuring that everyone, regardless of their financial circumstances, can participate and enjoy these community gatherings.
